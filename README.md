# HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard | Excel

## Project Overview
Analysed IBM's HR Analytics dataset of 1,470 employees to identify 
the root causes of a 16.12% attrition rate — above the industry 
benchmark of 12-14%.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Dashboard
- Data Cleaning & Helper Columns

## Dataset
- Source: IBM HR Analytics Dataset (Kaggle)
- Records: 1,470 employees
- Columns: 35 attributes

## Key Findings
- Overall attrition rate: 16.12% — above industry benchmark
- Sales Representatives had highest attrition at 39.76%
- Employees earning below $2,500/month showed 34.07% attrition
- First year employees at critical risk — 34.88% attrition rate
- Overtime employees are 3x more likely to leave at 30.53%
- 20-25 age group most at risk at 35.77%

## High Risk Employee Profile
Young (20-25) + Low Salary (Below $2,500) + Overtime (Yes) + Sales Representative + First Year = Highest attrition risk
